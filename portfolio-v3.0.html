How I work

I solve problems. AI is one of the ways.

I start with the person, not the feature: who does the work, what they repeat every day, and what their own records say. Then one pilot, a hard release gate, and only then scale.

The question I stopped asking"What do you do in your current software?"

You hear about missing buttons, and build a slightly better version of the same tool.

What I ask now, across 80+ calls"Walk me through your workday, morning to evening."

You hear about actions: calls answered, invoices checked, people chased. The repeated ones become candidates. Helpdesk AI and Payment Validator came from this.

Good problems repeat

Already known, happening daily, and worth it to the customer and the business.

No is my default

A customer asking for a button is usually describing a problem. I look for the problem.

Break it first

Every agent faces 50+ real scenarios, scored against the records it wrote, before a customer sees it.

It has to stick

A launch is not the finish line. The test is whether the customer works differently and keeps paying.
