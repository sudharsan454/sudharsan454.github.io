When the fix is a simpler product, not an agent

A US cleaning contractor was live on our field service product and unhappy. The team had built everything the customer asked for, and the customer still wasn't happy: too many steps, too many clicks, cases missed, and no single place to see what needed action.

What I did

The customer asked for me to join. I spent a month embedded with their team asking why each piece existed, who used it and what outcome they wanted, then cut what wasn't needed. The answer wasn't AI. It was standardising and simplifying what was already there.

Role
Product lead, embedded with the customer
Built
A Command Centre home page, and missing-field flags with steppers across vendors, proposals, RFQs and contracts

What I changed

  • Every screen says what is missing and what to do next. Empty required fields turn red, a blue bar names the button to click, and the Tab key jumps between the fields still to fill.
  • Steppers for every lifecycle. Vendor onboarding, proposals, RFQs and contracts each show where the record is and what is blocking it.
  • Contracts can't go out half-done. A contract created from a proposal lists what to fix before it can be published, like a missing billing cycle or credit note rule.
  • A home page that answers "what is waiting on us". The Command Centre shows everything that needs action, and one click opens the record.
  • Approvals in one place. They used to happen in three modules: invoices, credit notes and netted finance. Now only netted finance.

How I tested it

Contract changes in this business create invoices and credit notes, and mistakes cost real money. I ran about 250 test cases through the full life of each change and checked every record it created. Early runs flagged problems that weren't real, because the tests were missing context. Once that was fixed, they found 7 to 8 real bugs, and all of them were sorted.
